End-to-End Imitation Learning Pipeline

PHASE 1 Card Database & TokenGraph Ingestion Completed

Parsing 35,807 card entities from HDT XML dictionaries into an optimized SQLite cache with instant O(1) in-memory lookups (>22M/s). Integrated TokenGraph (8,661 cards, 6,069 relationships) to provide structural context.

PHASE 2 Replay Streaming & Option Oracle Contract Completed

Stream-reading 1,041 .hdtreplay archives without disk extraction. GameStateTracker reconstructs boards, zones, and captures Blizzard DebugPrintOptions error=NONE and SendOption events to produce Schema v2.

PHASE 3 Tactical Coach & Lethal Burst Detector Completed

Deterministic post-game evaluator (src/coach/analyzer.py) computing max burst lethal potential, mana efficiency, and tempo curves. Forms the verification baseline for detecting tactical blunders.

PHASE 4 Supervised Fine-Tuning (QLoRA ChatML) Blocked on CUDA

Autoregressive token generation trainer (src/llm/train_qlora.py) targeting Qwen-2.5-Coder-7B with 4-bit NF4 quantization. Currently held at gate pending isolated CUDA PyTorch environment.

PHASE 5 NLI Cross-Encoder / Jev Ranking Architecture Active

One-pass scoring of candidate decisions via ModernCE-large-nli (395M) and listwise linear projection. Bypasses autoregressive token generation latency (15ms vs 1200ms) with 0% candidate hallucination risk.

Analysis: The Action Collapse Failure Mode

In experiment tuning_tempered, class-weight power scaling (w^α) was tested across α ∈ {0.25, 0.50, 0.75} to resolve zero-accuracy on HERO_POWER.

Catastrophic Degradation: Boosting weights for rare classes severely penalized the dominant PLAY class, causing its accuracy to plummet from 26.06% down to 8.14%–15.70%. All weighted variants failed the non-inferiority gate.

Architectural Solution: Factorized Ranker

P(action) = P(type | state, legal_types) × P(candidate | type, state)

Decoupling macro-action type gating from within-type candidate ranking prevents rare-class loss inflation from contaminating card-play representations.

Post-Training Synergies (Negative Self-Distillation / DPO):

Human replays provide expert positive trajectories but lack counterfactual exploration. SabberStone simulates alternative candidate actions to deterministically verify whether non-chosen options lead to defeat, loss of lethal, or illegal board states — providing rigorous preference pairs ([Prompt, Chosen, Rejected]) for DPO training without hallucinated feedback.
